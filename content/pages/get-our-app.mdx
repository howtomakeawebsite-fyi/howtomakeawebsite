Our app is this website, saved to your phone's home screen. It gets its own icon and opens full screen, just like any other app.

## What is a web app?

- **It is free.** There is nothing to buy and no account to create.
- **No app store.** You add it straight from this website.
- **It takes up almost no space** on your phone.
- **It updates itself,** so you always see the latest [sermons](/sermons/), events and news.

## Step 1 in detail: saving the app to your phone

### iPhone or iPad

1. Open this page in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones, tap the **three dots** first, then **Share**.
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Check the name, then tap **Add**.

### Android phone

1. Open this page in **Chrome**.
2. If you see an **Install** button in step 1 above, tap it and you are done.
3. Otherwise, tap the **three dots** at the top right.
4. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then tap **Install**.

Using Samsung Internet? Tap the **menu** (three lines), then **Add page to**, then **Home screen**.

### Computer

- **Chrome or Microsoft Edge:** click the small **install** icon at the right end of the address bar, then **Install**.
- **Safari on a Mac:** click **File** in the menu bar, then **Add to Dock**, then **Add**.

## About notifications

On **iPhone and iPad**, notifications only work once the app is on your Home Screen and you have opened it from there (iOS 16.4 or newer). That is why saving and opening the app come first. On Android phones and computers, you can turn them on straight from this page or from the [Notifications](/notifications/) page.

To stop them, open the [Notifications](/notifications/) page and tap **Turn off**, or switch them off in your phone's settings.
